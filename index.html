What is real here
  • Past is two things: the past moon's slow average of what was seen, and the 20 weights, which are the summary of everything learned.
  • Present is the frame that just arrived, 64×64.
  • Future is the past read through the present: p = w · φ(x, x − x₋₁, mem). One linear filter shared by every pixel, solved by exact least squares with forgetting.
  • Walls show |camera − prediction|, the innovation, above the frame's own noise floor (3×3 average minus the frame mean). The innovation is the only signal that teaches.
  • Precision (on by default): the present moon shows a belief, p + K·(eye − p), with per-pixel K = 1 − R / max(running surprise, this frame's local surprise). On a still noisy room the belief is 7.5× closer to the clean scene than the raw camera, and a sudden new object passes through in its first frame (8% error).
  • Phase moon (off by default): its pre-registered test was killed, since the linear filter already gets 98.7% on smooth slow translation. It earns +3–4 points only on fast motion. Kept as an experiment.
  • The future moon moves to the peak of running squared surprise. That is the Kuulustelu rule: look where you expect the answer to split. It is programmed, not learned.
  • This is not a deep video model. It is the smallest model that has to beat copy-last-frame, and the skill meter says honestly whether it does. In tests it beats it by ~47% on a still noisy room and ~91% on smooth noiseless motion; real cameras vary.
  • Measured surprise: the dream does not melt to grey. The learned filter keeps the overall brightness, so the layout persists while fine detail dissolves.

Perjantai

A tiny brain that lives at 64×64 and keeps three moons.

pastwhat it has seen, slowly averaged, plus what it has learned presentthe frame arriving right now futurethe past, read through the present: its guess at the next frame

The walls light up only where that guess was wrong. Sit still and the room learns you and goes dark. Wave a hand and it lights up, and the future moon drifts over to look.

Everything runs in this page. Frames are never uploaded.
